# Modern SIS Problem Statement And Vision

## Purpose

The Modern Student Information System is intended to become the institution's unified academic operations platform. Its purpose is to connect administrative truth, learning activity, and governed decision support in one system so that students, advisors, faculty, and administrators can work from the same reliable picture of the student journey.

This is not a project to replace Moodle. It is a project to position Moodle correctly as the learning environment while the SIS remains the authoritative administrative system.

## The Core Problem

Higher education operations are fragmented across disconnected systems and manual workflows. Administrative records, teaching activity, and support processes are separated, which causes delays, duplicated effort, incomplete information, and late intervention.

In practice, this creates several institutional problems:

- Advisors must gather student context from multiple systems and still do not get a complete view.
- Students receive support too late because warning signs are noticed after academic damage is already visible.
- Administrative staff manually reconcile enrollments, course shells, and grades between the SIS and Moodle.
- Students rely on office queues and slow email replies for routine questions.
- Advising notes and support records are inconsistent, unstructured, and hard to recover later.
- Students in personal difficulty lack a discreet, low-friction, institutionally governed support pathway.
- Existing SIS-to-Moodle integrations are brittle and expensive to maintain.
- AI in education often lacks oversight, traceability, and institutional accountability.

## The Proposed Solution

The system addresses these problems through four coordinated solution pillars:

### 1. Unified Academic Record

The SIS remains the source of truth for students, courses, enrollments, grades, attendance, and advisory context. Moodle engagement signals are surfaced alongside SIS data so staff can act with complete context instead of partial information.

### 2. Standards-Based Moodle Integration

The platform provisions users, sections, enrollments, and official grades into Moodle using standard interfaces. Embedded SIS tools inside Moodle reduce context switching while keeping SIS-owned processes under SIS control.

### 3. Governed Decision Support

AI features are included only where they reduce friction without removing human accountability:

- student co-pilot for routine administrative questions
- staff summarisation for structured note drafting
- at-risk insights for earlier academic intervention
- opt-in wellbeing support under strict privacy and safeguarding controls

### 4. Auditability And Institutional Trust

The system is designed with oversight, audit logs, human review points, role boundaries, and governance controls so that institutional trust is preserved even when AI-assisted features are introduced.

## What Success Looks Like

If the system succeeds:

- students get answers and services faster
- advisors work proactively rather than reactively
- faculty enter key academic data once instead of twice
- admins stop spending time on repetitive reconciliation
- the institution gains a joined-up, auditable view of academic operations

## Product Thesis

The core thesis of the project is:

> If administrative truth, Moodle learning activity, and governed AI support are brought together in one controlled platform, the institution can reduce operational friction, intervene earlier, and support students more effectively without sacrificing accountability.
